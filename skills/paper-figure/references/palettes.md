# Palette library

Popular color combinations that work well in paper figures. Each line: an id, the colors from dark to light where that makes sense, and the mood. Pick 4 to 5 that fit the paper and the figure and show them to the user as a small preview figure.

A palette gives the hues for concepts and one accent. It does not give the background (always pure white) or the body text (always a neutral dark gray such as #1F2937). Very light colors in a palette (#EDF2F4, #FEFAE0, #F1FAEE and similar) are for fills or tints, never for the canvas.

## Calm and editorial (good for method overviews)

- `ocean-dusk`: #0D1B2A #1B263B #415A77 #778DA9 #E0E1DD. Cool slate blues, quiet and precise; one strong accent needs to come from elsewhere.
- `navy-mint`: #1D3557 #457B9D #A8DADC #F1FAEE #E63946. Navy and teal with one red accent; classic, clear for "ours vs. baseline".
- `steel-crimson`: #2B2D42 #8D99AE #EDF2F4 #EF233C #D90429. Charcoal and gray with a sharp red; good when one element must stand out.
- `twilight-mauve`: #22223B #4A4E69 #9A8C98 #C9ADA7 #F2E9E4. Muted violet to rose; soft, refined, low contrast between concepts.
- `ink-sky`: #023047 #219EBC #8ECAE6 #FFB703 #FB8500. Deep blue and sky blue with warm yellow and orange; two clear families.

## Warm and earthy (good for teasers and concept figures)

- `desert-teal`: #264653 #2A9D8F #E9C46A #F4A261 #E76F51. Teal to burnt orange; balanced, five distinct hues, very readable.
- `olive-clay`: #283618 #606C38 #FEFAE0 #DDA15E #BC6C25. Olive and clay; natural, mature.
- `sage-sand`: #CCD5AE #E9EDC9 #FEFAE0 #FAEDCD #D4A373. Very soft sage and tan; for fills and light illustrations, needs a dark partner for strokes.
- `coffee-brass`: #6F1D1B #BB9457 #432818 #99582A #FFE6A7. Dark browns and brass; strong, vintage.
- `burgundy-ochre`: #335C67 #FFF3B0 #E09F3E #9E2A2B #540B0E. Petrol blue, ochre, burgundy; rich and editorial.
- `wine-teal`: #5F0F40 #9A031E #FB8B24 #E36414 #0F4C5C. Wine red and orange against deep teal; dramatic.

## Bright and clear (good for small companion figures and plots)

- `cobalt-flag`: #780000 #C1121F #FDF0D5 #003049 #669BBC. Red and navy with a light cream tint; strong two-sided contrast.
- `sunset-navy`: #003049 #D62828 #F77F00 #FCBF49 #EAE2B7. Navy with red, orange and yellow; energetic, good for ordered categories.
- `lagoon-coral`: #0081A7 #00AFB9 #FDFCDC #FED9B7 #F07167. Turquoise and coral; fresh, friendly.
- `marine-signal`: #011627 #FDFFFC #2EC4B6 #E71D36 #FF9F1C. Near-black, teal, red, orange; high contrast, for plots with a few lines.
- `midnight-gold`: #000814 #001D3D #003566 #FFC300 #FFD60A. Deep blues with gold; one accent family, very clean.

## Vivid (use sparingly, usually for one accent only)

- `neon-violet`: #F72585 #7209B7 #3A0CA3 #4361EE #4CC9F0. Pink through violet to cyan; a gradient family, strong on screens.
- `pop-primary`: #FFBE0B #FB5607 #FF006E #8338EC #3A86FF. Saturated pop colors; too loud for most papers, fine for one highlight.
- `berry-fire`: #390099 #9E0059 #FF0054 #FF5400 #FFBD00. Indigo to yellow; bold, for one accented scale.

## Pastel (fills and backgrounds of regions, not strokes)

- `cotton-candy`: #CDB4DB #FFC8DD #FFAFCC #BDE0FE #A2D2FF. Lilac, pink and baby blue; pair with a dark version of each hue for lines and text.
